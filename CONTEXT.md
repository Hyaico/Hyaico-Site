# HYAICO Company Website — Design Direction

## Intent
HYAICO is a company of innovation; its first product is Just Cooked (justcooked.app). The site must say "innovative, thinks differently, building the future" through how it looks and moves, not through copy. Almost no words. One continuous scroll-driven 3D story that mixes particles with real geometry and makes sense from scene to scene: the Earth → into its DNA → out to a galaxy → back home at sunrise (Just Cooked, where its download links live) → night, the stars gather into HYAICO → the ending.

The copy, exactly: "Building *what's next.*" (hero and ending), "Stretching *what can be done.*" (DNA), "And if it's done, *it can be better.*" (galaxy), "For when *food feels hard.*" (Just Cooked). Every title types itself out at a constant pace (time-based, never scrubbed by the scroll).

Things explicitly rejected in earlier versions and not to return: paragraphs of explanatory copy, "AI company" framing, a bright 3D object behind the hero title, a hero title close to the globe, a plain sphere of particles, a wireframe, grey or stylised dot-grid globe (it is the real Earth: Blue Marble, real countries with borders), particles floating on the globe for no reason (they are the world's city lights), small rings that don't belong on an atom (latitude circles, connection arcs: removed), plain thin orbit hoops (orbits have a glow band, drifting colour, streaming charge and flared electrons, and pass in front of and behind the Earth), a helix that forms in the centre and slides over, a helix drawn as ribbons in particle fuzz, plastic tubes, or with orbs between segments (it is light: twisted ribbon backbones with a core line and bright edges, tapered base pairs in two blues), a galaxy on flat black, particle effects on the chrome HYAICO, a small, washed-out, hazy or pixelated phone, circles or rings of light, dust clouds, starfields, light columns or a grid floor around the phone, lens ghosts (they read as circles; the glare is a bloom, a horizon streak and soft rays only), a flat or dusky Just Cooked backdrop (it is an azure golden hour over a sea that runs to the true horizon), still clouds (they drift, in perspective), clouds that read as haze, any line or band where the sea meets the sky (the sky is a screen-space quad that no clipping can cut), a sea lighter or darker than the sky while the stage fades (the sea is drawn first and mirrors the whole sky; the sky lies on the far plane and only fills what the sea leaves), a sun glint that reads as a spot on the ocean, bloom that blacks out the scene backgrounds (it adds light, never coverage), a muddy brown fade into the sunset (the stage arrives as night with stars and warms into the golden hour as the sun rises), the sun hidden behind the phone, a phone that rises edge-on, copy that is hard to read over the sky (it sits on a pane of dark glass), copy hugging the far left of the phone beat, eyebrows or blinking dots on any beat, screen-indicator bars, a flat or blown-out chrome, plain black backgrounds (each scene has its own background), any fixed wash that reads as a horizontal split or a two-tone background, soft blurry "highlight" reveals in the loader (it is a white laser scan), a plain loader background (it is a breathing nebula with drifting stars), a hard rectangle behind the loader logo (never a filter and a mask on one element), a loader counter that changes width, any burst, flash or explosion on hand-over (the stars rushing past at the hand-over is the arrival, not an explosion), anything of the next scene visible before the veil lifts, a Just Cooked button in the nav, a coloured-tile favicon, an invented contact email or roadmap teasers, a trademark line in the footer, a phone that lingers behind the ending, a product CTA as the final frame, a particle version of the wordmark as the ending, an eyebrow or small logo above the closing line, a closing line too high above the wordmark, the typed "Just Cooked" name instead of the logotype, the tagline "Home cooking, made easy", a laggy or offset cursor, anything that hitches the first time it appears, and asset links without a cache-busting query.

## Visual system
- **Theme:** near-black base (`#06060a`) with a background per scene behind the (see-through) canvas (deep space with a blue glow under the Earth; a blue glow behind the DNA; violet, rose and blue nebula colour round the galaxy; deep night blue for the stars and the chrome; a soft violet light under the ending), cross-faded by opacity. Film grain at very low opacity. The Just Cooked beat is the one warm, bright moment.
- **Type:** Geist (headlines, weight 500, tight tracking) with Instrument Serif italic for the accent words, Geist Mono for the few labels. Keep this pairing.
- **Accents:** violet `#7c5cff`, cyan `#45e3ff`, Just Cooked orange `#e94b1f`, warm `#ff8a5c`, cream `#fbf6ec` (from the Just Cooked site's tokens).
- **Logos:** `HyaicoWhite.svg` everywhere for HYAICO (nav, loader, particles, chrome, ending); `JustCookedLogo.svg` (the rebranded logotype) in the phone beat. Favicon: the white H from the wordmark on a transparent background.

## Structure (single page)
1. **Loader** — a canvas sky (a nebula of azure, violet and rose that breathes slowly; stars drifting toward the viewer) behind the wordmark in polished chrome (a horizon-banded metal gradient masked by the logo, the same metal it becomes at the end), scanned in by a white laser with an anamorphic flare; a glow pulses from a blurred copy; a light sweeps across the finished word; a counter line with a fixed-width counter. At the hand-over the stars accelerate into short streaks as the sky and veil fade, the logo lifts away, and the Earth is already rising. Nothing bursts.
2. **Pinned story** (900% scroll):
   - **The Earth** (radius 2.6, centred just below the bottom edge; smaller and higher on phones; Europe and Africa face the viewer first and it turns slowly eastward): NASA Blue Marble by day lit from the upper left, Black Marble city lights on the night side to the right, Natural Earth borders (and faint coasts), a warm terminator band, a broad faint sheen of sunlight on the oceans (never a spot), a cloud shell, an atmosphere at the limb and an outer glow. The particles are placed by the city-light map, gold, brightest on the night side. The atom: three orbits (1.42× the radius), 60° apart, oriented so each passes in front of the Earth on one side and behind it on the other; each a fine line plus a soft band, colour drifting cyan↔violet, charge streaming along it into a comet tail behind a flared electron; far halves and electrons behind the Earth are dimmer. Title above, typed after the loader.
   - **Into the DNA**: the camera dives toward the Earth while its lights lift off; the Earth falls away; the helix (two twisted ribbons with a bright centre line and edges, tapered base pairs in light and deep blue with a gap where each pair meets, pulses up the strands, the far side dimmer) grows from the bottom as the particles arrive bottom-first. Right of the title on desktop with a slight lean; below it and smaller on phones.
   - **Out to the galaxy**: the helix unwinds (outer particles turn further) as the camera pulls far back and up; warm core, blue-white arms, rose nebulae, stars, flares, nebula haze; the title spans the top.
   - **Back home**: the galaxy core arcs down to the horizon and becomes the sun; the stage arrives in night colours with stars (matching space) and warms into the golden hour as the sun rises to sit half-set on the horizon. The sky is a screen-space quad computing each pixel's view ray: peach horizon, dusty rose, azure overhead, a luminous mist, the sun with corona, clouds from a panorama painted once with seamless periodic noise and drifted per row in proportion to height (perspective), cirrus, night colours and procedural stars by a night factor. The sea is the `Water` addon to the true horizon. Lens glare: bloom, anamorphic horizon streak, soft rays — no ghosts. The phone rises out of the water facing us. The copy sits on a pane of dark glass (blurred backdrop, soft border) in from the left: logotype, the typed tagline in warm orange italics, cream App Store and glass Google Play buttons, justcooked.app; on phones the pane sits at the bottom over a deepening dusk.
   - **Night falls**: the sky deepens, the sun sinks below the sea, stars come out, the phone sinks, the copy leaves; the stage fades to the night background as the particle stars gather into HYAICO; the chrome takes over (horizon studio, travelling light).
   Only HUD: a bottom-centre scroll cue that hides on first scroll.
3. **Ending** — the chrome glides down and becomes the faint masked wordmark (even tone, bottom fade, light sweep, capped at 1400px); above it, a little below the centre of the free space, "Building *what's next*." types itself out and then draws its violet-to-cyan underline; a soft violet light behind it; a bar with © HYAICO and back to top.

## Motion
- Scroll scrubs the 3D story and the beats' exits; titles type themselves out on their own clock when their beat is reached (and again if you scroll back), behind a glowing caret that blinks a few times and goes.
- Forms blend with purpose: lights lift off the Earth, the DNA unwinds into the galaxy, the galaxy's light becomes the sun, the stars become HYAICO; the camera dives in, pulls back, comes down to the sea.
- Particles carry individual parallax depth under the pointer (the city lights stay put); the camera moves only slightly and stops moving as the ending lands. The phone follows the pointer with the Just Cooked site's resting tilt.
- Cursor: dot pinned to the pointer, ring follows tightly, crosshair centred.
- Respects `prefers-reduced-motion` (loader skipped, no typing, forms still follow scroll).

## Performance
- Particles: 60k desktop / 28k phones; helix, galaxy and dust forms built after the first frame; city-light positions sampled once from the night map. Bloom only on fine-pointer devices, only on the particle layer, through a 4× multisampled target at 1×. The sea's reflection renders at 1024×512 (384×768 on phones). Phones get the 2k day map and 1k clouds.
- Warm-up under the loader: `compileAsync` for the particle scene (composer target) and the product scene (screen and a reflection-like target), `initTexture` for every texture as it loads, the chrome compiled as soon as it exists; the later text painted once behind the veil.
- The cloud panorama is painted once (2048×768 desktop, 1024×640 phones), repainted only if the view needs a wider period or the sun moves; the sky does a single lookup.
- The particle layer is skipped while the sky fully covers it and after the ending; transparent sprites and stars are culled. Scene backgrounds are CSS layers animated by opacity only.
- Scroll-scrubbed DOM motion is opacity and transforms only. Minified three.js, modulepreload, trimmed fonts. Pixel ratio capped at 1.5. Rendering pauses when the tab is hidden.
- No build step; pinned CDN versions.

## Constraints
- Static files only, deployable to GitHub Pages; relative asset paths; serve over HTTP locally (ES modules).
