# HYAICO Company Website — Design Direction

## Intent
HYAICO is a company of innovation; its first product is Just Cooked (justcooked.app). The site must say "innovative, thinks differently, built for the future" through how it looks and moves, not through copy. Almost no words. One continuous scroll-driven 3D story that mixes particles with real geometry, with Just Cooked introduced naturally in the middle-late stretch (that is where its download links live) and HYAICO itself as the closing image.

Things explicitly rejected in earlier versions and not to return: paragraphs of explanatory copy, "AI company" framing, a bright 3D object behind the hero title, a hero title sitting close to the globe (it sits well above it), a plain sphere of particles, a wireframe-only globe or a dark grey globe as the hero (it is an Earth with blue oceans and green land), particles that merely float on the globe for no reason (they are the continents, and the arcs carry pulses), a helix that forms in the centre and slides over (it forms where it lives), a helix drawn as flat additive ribbons wrapped in particle fuzz, or as glossy plastic tubes, or with orbs between the segments (it is light: a hologram shader, all shades of blue, continuous backbones, no nodes), a galaxy on flat black (it sits in deep space: stars, flares, a faint nebula haze), particle effects on or coming off the chrome HYAICO, a small, washed-out, hazy or pixelated phone (anti-aliasing on; no bloom or tone mapping on its screen; a screen image that pokes past the rounded glass), circles or rings of light and dust clouds around the phone, a starfield or light column behind the phone, a Tron-style grid floor, a flat dark cyclorama for the Just Cooked beat (it is an azure golden hour with clouds, and the phone stands on water), a dusky indigo/magenta sky (too dark; they asked for azure), clouds that read as haze (they need shape, shadow and lit edges), a horizon band or floor edge that reads as a horizontal split, a dark line where water meets sky (the reflection's clear colour), a bright strip there (water extended behind the sky), the sun hidden behind the phone, a phone that rises edge-on like a floating bar, a jumbled atom (crossing hoops, too many arcs and grid lines), and orbits that hug the globe without visibly going around it (each orbit passes in front of the globe on one side and behind it on the other), a sea that ends at a visible edge (it runs to the true horizon), a band where sea meets sky from haze or tone mapping that the sky doesn't share, a flat grey or blown-out white chrome (it needs the horizon studio), copy hugging the far left of the phone beat or sitting small and grey, a phone reflection running under the copy on phones, an "Introducing" eyebrow with a blinking dot on the Just Cooked beat, a HYAICO eyebrow with a dot on the hero, screen-indicator bars under the Just Cooked tagline, particle-only scenes with nothing else in them, a scene with no purpose, a scene rail or scene labels, soft blurry "highlight" reveals in the loader (the laser scan is the one that works; its beam is white), anything that hitches the first time it appears (shaders are compiled during the loader), blur filters in scroll-scrubbed animations or backdrop blur over the live canvas, a hard rectangle behind the loader logo (never combine a filter with a mask on one element), a text-shadow on a title that is split into line masks (the mask clips it into a visible box), a loader counter whose width changes and shrinks the bar, any burst, flash or explosion on hand-over, anything of the next scene visible before the veil lifts, a Just Cooked button in the nav, a coloured-tile favicon (it is the white H from the wordmark, with PNG fallbacks and a cache-busting query), an invented contact email or roadmap teasers, a phone that lingers behind the ending, a product CTA as the final frame, a particle version of the wordmark as the ending (the faint masked wordmark with the light sweep is the one they like), an eyebrow or small logo above the closing line, a closing line that is not centred in the space above the wordmark, a wordmark with a bright-middle gradient (it is one even tone), any fixed colour wash on the page background (it reads as a horizontal split; the page is flat black and the stage paints all colour), the typed "Just Cooked" name instead of the logotype, the tagline "Home cooking, made easy" (it is "For when food feels hard."), a laggy or offset cursor, and stylesheet or script links without a cache-busting query (the owner's browser served stale CSS once).

## Visual system
- **Theme:** flat near-black (`#06060a`) everywhere, film grain at very low opacity (no blend mode). The Just Cooked beat is the one warm, bright moment: an azure golden hour over water.
- **Type:** Geist (headlines, weight 500, tight tracking) with Instrument Serif italic for the accent words, Geist Mono for the few labels. Keep this pairing.
- **Accents:** violet `#7c5cff`, cyan `#45e3ff`, Just Cooked orange `#e94b1f`, warm `#ff8a5c`, cream `#fbf6ec` (from the Just Cooked site's tokens).
- **Logos:** `HyaicoWhite.svg` everywhere for HYAICO (nav, loader, particles, chrome, ending); `JustCookedLogo.svg` (the rebranded logotype) in the phone beat. Favicon: the white H from the wordmark on a transparent background (`favicon.svg`, `favicon-32.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png`).

## Structure (single page)
1. **Loader** — real wordmark scanned in by a thin white laser (tight feathered mask), glow pulse from a blurred copy, counter line with a fixed-width counter; the logo lifts away and the veil clears while the Earth rises into place behind it. Nothing bursts.
2. **Pinned story** (900% scroll) — forms held in order, each with geometry of its own, every change swirling into the next:
   - the Earth (radius 2.6, centred just below the bottom edge so its upper half shows; smaller and higher on phones; body textured from a land mask: blue oceans, green land, lit upper-left, blue rim; the particles sit on the continents with a tiny parallax so the land holds its shape; a dot grid on the land; six arcs with pulses of light that fade before they reach the title; faint latitude/longitude lines; the far side hidden behind the body) with an atom of three orbits (radius 1.42× the planet) laid out like the symbol, 60° apart, oriented for the hero camera so each passes in front of the globe on one side and behind it on the other: one sweeps across the face, two loop over the shoulders; their far halves are dimmer; each carries an electron trailing a comet tail, hidden or dimmed while it is behind. All under "Built for *what's next*." set high above it. The planet sets below the frame as its particles leave.
   - DNA helix of light (hologram shader: bright rims, pulses up the strands, additive; two continuous backbones, rungs in a light and a deep blue, no nodes; grown with clipping planes from the bottom up as the particles arrive bottom-first, dissolved the same way) right of "Things that *should exist*." on desktop with a slight lean, below it and smaller on phones
   - spiral galaxy in deep space under "Ideas, *in motion*.": particles tinted from a warm core to blue-white arms with rose nebulae, a bright core, thousands of distant stars, fourteen flare stars, a faint violet/peach/blue/rose nebula haze. On the way to the stage the core swells into a sun, rises a little and settles onto the horizon exactly where the sky's sun sits; the sky's corona warms beneath it first (`uSunGlow`), then its disc takes over (`uSunDisc`) as the sprite fades; the stars stay until the sky has covered them.
   - the stage: a sea that runs to the true horizon (the `Water` addon on a plane kilometres long, the camera a little above it looking almost level, far plane 2400) under a sky dome shaded by view direction: peach on the horizon, dusty rose, azure overhead, a luminous mist on the horizon, a half-set sun just beside the phone, and clouds on a layer above the sea so perspective draws them out toward the horizon — blue-violet in shadow, gold and rose on the side facing the low sun, silver linings near it, pale cirrus higher up. The clouds are painted once into a panorama render target and looked up (drifting very slowly sideways). The far sea mirrors the horizon exactly (no extra haze, no tone mapping on the sea, since the sky has none), so there is no line where they meet. A soft dusk shade sits on the copy's side of the screen (on phones, a deepening dusk at the bottom under the copy). The phone rises out of the water once the water is fully there, facing us, and stands on it. Beside it the Just Cooked logotype, "For when *food feels hard*." as a two-line display headline with warm serif italics, a cream App Store button, a glass Google Play button and justcooked.app, big and close to the phone, built up piece by piece. The phone is 72vh tall on desktop (45vh on phones), built to the Just Cooked site's spec with its screen-only crops on a rounded screen, drawn in its own anti-aliased pass with no bloom or tone mapping; the screen cross-fades Home → Next Meal → Recipe while it is up.
   - HYAICO written in particles, then the bevelled chrome HYAICO takes over completely — roughness 0.03, a little clearcoat, lit by a procedural chrome studio (bright sky over a dark floor with a hot glint on the horizon, all within a few hundredths of y because the flat faces reflect within ±3° of straight back; the horizon sits low on the letters and rocks gently) plus a travelling light that exists for the metal only; the particles are gone while it is metal
   Only HUD: a bottom-centre scroll cue that hides on first scroll.
3. **Ending** — the chrome HYAICO glides down and becomes the faint masked wordmark (even tone, bottom fade, light sweep, capped at 1400px wide) as it fades in; in the space above it, a little below its centre, a big "Built for *what's next*." draws a violet-to-cyan underline; the background is the page's flat black; a bar with © HYAICO, the trademark line and back to top.

## Motion
- Scroll scrubs everything (Lenis + ScrollTrigger): forms hold, then blend with a swirl; camera moves between keyframes; titled beats rise out of line masks and leave upward; the Just Cooked block staggers in.
- Hero title appears with masked line reveals (pre-split before the hand-over).
- Particles carry individual parallax depth under the pointer; the camera moves only slightly and stops moving as the ending lands. The phone follows the pointer with the Just Cooked site's resting tilt.
- Cursor: dot pinned to the pointer, ring follows tightly, crosshair centred.
- Respects `prefers-reduced-motion` (loader skipped, forms still follow scroll).

## Performance
- Particles: 60k desktop / 28k phones, additive points in a custom shader; helix, galaxy and dust forms are built after the first frame. Bloom only on fine-pointer devices, only on the particle layer, through a 4× multisampled target at 1×. The sea's reflection renders at 1024×512 (384×768 on phones).
- Warm-up: on the first frames (under the loader) `compileAsync` compiles the particle scene for the composer's target and the product scene for both the screen and a render target like the sea's reflection; every texture is uploaded with `initTexture` as it loads; the chrome is compiled as soon as its geometry exists.
- The clouds are painted into a panorama (2048×768 desktop, 1024×640 phones) on the first frame and on layout changes; the dome does one texture lookup.
- The particle layer is not drawn while the sky fully covers it or after the ending; screen-sized sprites and the stars are culled while transparent.
- The later text beats (DNA, galaxy, Just Cooked) are shown for two frames behind the loader's veil so their first paint happens there.
- Scroll-scrubbed DOM motion is opacity and transforms only; the animated elements are their own layers; no backdrop blur over the canvas.
- Loading: minified three.js, modulepreload for three and the scene, trimmed font weights, no unused plugins. Renderer anti-aliasing on; pixel ratio capped at 1.5. Rendering pauses when the tab is hidden.
- No build step; pinned CDN versions.

## Constraints
- Static files only, deployable to GitHub Pages; relative asset paths; serve over HTTP locally (ES modules).
